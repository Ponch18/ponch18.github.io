About

I build business software on Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, and today most of my work answers one question: how can AI agents make an ERP truly easier to use?

I am a Senior Software Developer at EOS Online, where I work on So Smart: a ready-to-use Business Central solution for small businesses that goes live in about 10 days, with no implementation project. It is available on Microsoft AppSource in Italy and the United States.

I work AI first. Coding agents are part of my daily workflow, and I turn our team's conventions into reusable skills and plugins, so everyone ships faster with consistent quality.

Before EOS Online I spent more than six years at EOS Solutions, a Microsoft Dynamics partner with over 100 Business Central apps on AppSource, where I grew from Software Developer to Team Leader. I started my career in 2013 at Merlo Group. Today I also mentor new developers on AL and Business Central.
